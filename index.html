What Ovenbird does

Offline vector maps

Download a region pack once, then get a full trail map — shelters, water sources, fire towers, and more — with zero connectivity required.

Live trail stats

Distance, elevation gain/loss, pace, and grade, tracked from your phone's own GPS and sensors while you hike.

On-device AI chat

Ask Ovenbird v1, Ovenbird's on-device assistant, about what's around you — every answer is grounded in your downloaded pack data, and nothing you type ever leaves your phone.

Frequently asked questions

Does Ovenbird work without cell service?

Yes — once a region pack and the AI model are downloaded, the map, GPS stats, and chat all work fully offline.

Why does the chat need a downloaded model?

Ovenbird v1 runs entirely on your device so it works with zero connectivity; that's why it needs a one-time download first.

Can I use my own reference files in chat?

Yes — attach a file from a Resources folder using the + button in the chat input, and the AI will use it to ground its answers.

Does the app track my location remotely?

No — GPS position is only ever used on-device to show your position and ground chat answers. It is never uploaded anywhere.

Do I need to create an account?

No. We believe in immediate utility without friction — open the app, download your regional pack, and hit the trail.

Why do I need to grant location permissions?

Location permission (When In Use) is required strictly so the map view can render your position relative to the trail, elevation contours, and nearby landmarks.

Where does the trail and shelter data come from?

Map data and trail geometries are sourced from OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), processed into optimized regional vector packs for fast offline rendering.

How do I download the AI model or a region pack again after deleting it?

Open the side panel and go to Models (for Ovenbird v1) or Packs (for a region). The AI model can be re-downloaded anytime at no cost. A region pack, though, is a one-time download on the free tier — if you delete it, you'll need Ovenbird Pro to download another. Pro users can re-download any previously-installed pack anytime.

How do I cancel or manage my Ovenbird Pro subscription?

In the app, go to Settings → Account & Subscription → Manage/Cancel Subscription, which opens Apple's own subscription management screen. You can also manage it directly in Settings → [your Apple ID] → Subscriptions on your device.

I found a bug, or the app crashed — what should I include in my report?

Use the form below and include your device model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue — that's usually enough for us to reproduce it quickly. A screenshot helps too, if you can attach one when you reply to our confirmation email.

Privacy Policy

Effective July 31, 2026 · Last updated July 31, 2026

Your privacy is our highest priority. Ovenbird is built offline-first: your personal information and location stay on your device, under your control.

Zero cloud data collection

We do not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal data. There's no account, no login, and no user database — everything you do in the app, including waypoints, downloaded map packs, and preferences, is stored locally on your device.

Location data

Location Services power your live position on offline maps and altitude/route tracking. Coordinates are processed strictly in real time, on your hardware — never uploaded, shared, or linked to an online identifier.

On-device AI

Ovenbird v1, Ovenbird's AI assistant, answers questions using your downloaded pack data entirely on-device. Nothing you type, and no location context, is ever used to train a model or shared with a third-party AI vendor.

Network connections

The app connects to the internet only to download region packs, the AI model, and to fetch live weather/place data from the public sources listed below. None of these requests carry personal identifiers or your location.

No tracking

We don't use advertising SDKs, cross-app tracking frameworks, or data brokers of any kind.

This support page

If you use the contact form above, your name, email, and message are sent to us and to our form processor, Web3Forms, solely so we can respond to you.

Terms of Service

Effective July 31, 2026

Secondary navigation only

Ovenbird is intended for secondary navigational assistance only. Backcountry environments carry inherent risk — always carry a physical topographic map, a magnetic compass, proper gear, and adequate supplies. Never rely solely on a phone for wilderness safety.

Open data & attribution

Map features, trails, and regional geometry are derived from OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License (ODbL).

Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Ovenbird's developers are not liable for damages resulting from use of, or inability to use, this app — including getting lost, injury, property damage, or search-and-rescue expenses.

Ovenbird Pro subscription

Ovenbird Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed through your Apple ID at the price shown at purchase. It renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends, and can be managed any time in Settings → [your Apple ID] → Subscriptions, or from within the app. No refund is issued for the unused portion of a period if you cancel partway through — canceling stops future renewals only. Free-tier limits apply automatically if a subscription lapses or is canceled: one saved trip at a time, 7 AI chat messages per day, and one region pack total (this does not reset, even if the pack is later deleted).
